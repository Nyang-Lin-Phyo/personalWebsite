High-Speed Research Network (HSRN)

Audio Systems Team Lead • NYU Tandon

Refactored a legacy distributed real-time audio receiver from a dual-stream architecture into a scalable multi-sender pipeline capable of supporting synchronized audio from globally distributed performers.

Designed lock-free audio processing pipelines with per-sender jitter buffers, dynamic stream subscription, and real-time audio mixing, allowing new performers to join without interrupting active sessions.

Resolved long-standing plugin lifecycle crashes, simplified stream management, and established a maintainable architecture that significantly improved reliability for large-scale collaborative live audio applications.
