High-Speed Research Network (HSRN)

Audio Systems Team Lead • NYU Tandon

03

Scaling Laws for Transformer Language Models on SVG Code

Investigated scaling behavior of decoder-only transformer models trained on structured SVG code, evaluating 10 model variants under Standard Parameterization and Maximal Update Parameterization (µP).

Conducted systematic learning rate sweeps and cross-width transfer experiments, demonstrating stable zero-shot hyperparameter transfer under µP and unstable scaling behavior under standard parameterization.

Trained up to 91M-parameter models on a 4K BPE SVG dataset, achieving 1.57 test perplexity with 100% SVG validity and fully renderable outputs.
